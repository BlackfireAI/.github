# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume the person you are replying to is acting in good faith and
knows things you do not.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, and demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background,
nationality, language or level of experience.

We work on low-resource languages, and people who contribute here come from very different
technical backgrounds. A question that seems basic to you may be the first time someone has
had anyone to ask. Treat it accordingly.

## Scope

This applies in our repositories, issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere
someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems to [hello@blackfire.com.np](mailto:hello@blackfire.com.np). Reports stay
between the reporter and the people handling them.

We will respond in proportion to what happened. That ranges from asking someone to change
their behaviour, through removing comments, to blocking an account. We will say what we
decided and why.
