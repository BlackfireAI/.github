# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email [hello@blackfire.com.np](mailto:hello@blackfire.com.np) with the details. Do not open
a public issue for a security problem.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what an attacker could do with it. We will
confirm receipt within five working days and tell you what we intend to do.

## Scope

This covers the code in our public repositories and our published models.

For models, we are interested in reports of training data leakage, and of inputs that cause
a model to crash or hang a service rather than simply transcribe badly. Ordinary
transcription errors are accuracy issues, not security issues, and belong in a normal
issue.

Our hosted products are out of scope here. Report those to the same address and say which
product.
